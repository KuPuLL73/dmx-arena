# DMX Arena — Telegram copy

## English

Welcome to DMX Arena!

Open the app and link your Pixel World UID. Build a loadout with your skins, complete quests and earn DMX in clan battles.

Tap “Open arena” to get started.

## Russian

Добро пожаловать в DMX Arena!

Открой приложение и привяжи UID Pixel World. Выбери свои скины для сета, выполняй задания и зарабатывай DMX в клановых битвах.

Нажми «Открыть арену», чтобы начать.

## Behavior

The full welcome is sent on the first private `/start`. Repeated starts show a short return message. Russian Telegram locales use Russian; other locales fall back to English. Delivery is deduplicated through a transactional claim and retry lease.
